# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Standards
Be respectful, constructive, and professional. No trolling, insults, personal attacks, or publishing others private information.

## Enforcement
Report violations to admin@rugmunch.io. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or access for violations.
