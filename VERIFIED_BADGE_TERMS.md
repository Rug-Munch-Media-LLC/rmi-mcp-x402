## 1. WHAT IS THE RUG MUNCH VERIFIED BADGE?

## 2. WHAT THE BADGE MEANS

A verified token has:

- ✅ Passed all 32 RMI security detectors
- ✅ Has no critical or high-severity findings
- ✅ Has a clean ownership structure (no hidden mint functions, no blacklisted deployer)
- ✅ Has reasonable tokenomics (verified by our team)
- ✅ Has had its smart contract source code manually reviewed
- ✅ Has had its deployer wallet history analyzed
- ✅ Is monitored continuously ($50/mo retainer)

## 3. WHAT THE BADGE DOES NOT MEAN

- ❌ Endorsement of the project's team, marketing, or claims

The badge certifies a **point-in-time security assessment**, not a permanent approval.

## 5. OUR ANALYSIS PROCESS (FULL TRANSPARENCY)

When you submit for verification, our team will:

### 5.1 Run All 32 Detectors

The token contract and deployment are scanned through every detector in our suite. **Every finding is documented**, including:
- Detector name
- Severity (critical/high/medium/low)
- Evidence (specific code, transaction, or pattern)
- Risk assessment
- Recommendation

### 5.2 Manual Code Review

Our security team manually reviews the smart contract source code, looking for:
- Hidden mint functions
- Proxy upgrade patterns
- Owner-only functions that could be abused
- Backdoors or honeypots
- Unusual tokenomics
- Comparison to known scam patterns

### 5.3 Deployer History Analysis

The deployer wallet and all related wallets are traced:
- Previous contracts deployed
- Transaction patterns
- Connections to known bad actors
- Holdings and movements
- Age and activity

### 5.4 Team Wallet Verification

ALL team member wallets are checked:
- Holdings of the token being verified
- Holdings of related tokens
- Historical trading patterns
- Any suspicious activity

### 5.5 Final Report

You receive a **detailed report** with:
- Every detector result
- Every manual finding
- Every risk identified
- Our honest assessment
- Final verdict (verified, verified with caveats, or rejected)
